About Modern Healthcare

Modern Healthcare is an intelligent illness prediction platform powered by Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence. The system analyzes user symptoms and predicts possible illnesses quickly and efficiently using a trained Random Forest model. This project is designed to provide a fast, user-friendly, and modern healthcare assistance experience through AI technology.
